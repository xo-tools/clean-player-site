Clean Player for Streaming: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short: Clean Player does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any information about you. Everything it keeps stays in your own browser.

What the extension does

Clean Player for Streaming is a browser extension that mutes profanity and hides scenes you choose while you watch Netflix, HBO Max or Prime Video. It runs only on those services' video pages.

Information processed on your device

  • Subtitle text of the title you are watching is read inside the page to find words to mute. It is processed in your browser and never transmitted.
  • The title's ID (for example the number in a Netflix address) is used to look up the filters saved for that title.
  • Your settings, word list and filters (time ranges with an action such as "mute" or "skip") are saved with the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer.

What is not collected

The extension has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. It does not send your viewing activity, browsing history, personal details or any other data to the developer or to anyone else. It does not use cookies.

On Prime Video, the extension downloads the title's subtitle file from the same address the Prime Video player uses, so that profanity can be found without turning subtitles on. That request goes to Amazon, as when the player loads subtitles itself, and no information is added to it.

Files you import or export

Filter files you export are saved to your computer by your browser. Files you import are read locally. The extension never uploads them.

Deleting your data

Delete individual titles on the extension's Manage filters & words page, or remove the extension from your browser to erase everything it stored.

Children

The extension collects no information from anyone, including children.
